A skribbl.io alternative for games with your own people

If you have played skribbl.io, you already know how Drawdle works: one person draws, everyone else guesses in the chat, and the quickest right answer scores the most. Drawdle is made for playing with people you know, on whatever device they have with them.

Try Drawdle free

What you get

  • Private rooms. Only people with your link or room code can join.
  • No ads, no sign-up. Type a name, pick an avatar, play.
  • Up to 16 players in one room.
  • Made for phones. The drawing and the guess box stay on screen together, even with the keyboard open.
  • Your seat is kept. If your connection drops mid-game, you rejoin where you were.
  • Host controls. Rounds, draw time, hints, how many words to choose from, word packs and difficulty.
  • Your own words. Up to 500, mixed in or used on their own.
  • Word packs you won't find elsewhere, like Desi and Corporate slang.

Moving your group over

  1. Open drawdle.in, type your name and create a room.
  2. Paste your old custom word list into “Your own words”. Comma-separated lists work as they are.
  3. Pick the rounds and draw time you are used to, then share the invite link.

A player spoiling the game? The host can remove them, and the other players can vote them out.

When skribbl.io is the better pick

Drawdle has no public lobby. If you want to drop into a game with strangers at any hour, skribbl.io is still the place. If you would rather play with your friends, family, classmates or team, that is what Drawdle is for.

And if your group likes chaos more than scores, Gartic Phone is worth a look: drawings get passed along like a game of telephone. Drawdle sticks to the classic: draw it, guess it.

Questions

Is Drawdle free like skribbl.io?

Yes, completely, and without ads.

Can I use my skribbl.io custom words?

Yes. Paste them into “Your own words” in the room settings, separated by commas or one per line. Up to 500 words.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The game screen is laid out for phones first, in portrait or landscape.

Is Drawdle related to skribbl.io?

No. It is an independent game that plays in a similar way.
