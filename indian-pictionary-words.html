Questions

What are good Indian words for Pictionary?

Food is the easiest start: ladoo, roti, kulfi. Festivals like Holi and Diwali are just as quick. Street food like samosa and vada pav, and cricket words like sixer, take a little more. Save the Bollywood titles and Hinglish ideas like “jugaad” for when everyone is warmed up.

Can I use these words for dumb charades?

Yes. The Bollywood films are made for it, and most of the food, festival and cricket words act out as well as they draw.

Are all of these words in the online game?

Yes, every one: together they are Drawdle's Desi pack. Switch it on in the room settings, on its own or with other packs.
