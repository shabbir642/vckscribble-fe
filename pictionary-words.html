How the words are rated

Every word is rated for how easily it draws, not for how rare it is. Everyone knows the word “synergy”. Nobody can draw it.

  • Easy draws itself. One shape and a detail or two: a cat, an umbrella, a rocket. A stick figure and a circle will do.
  • Medium needs a second object or some setup. An alarm clock is a clock with bells on top, and “walk the dog” needs a person, a leash and a dog.
  • Hard is abstract or easily confused. “Procrastinate” and “gravity” have no shape at all, an antelope looks like a gazelle, and hummus looks like any other dip.

A Drawdle room deals words by these ratings. With the difficulty on Easy you only get Easy words, Mixed is mostly Easy with some Medium, and Hard means anything goes, Hard words included.

Questions

How many words are there?

Who decides whether a word is easy or hard?

The ratings were set by hand, word by word, as each one went into the game. They answer one question: how quickly can an ordinary doodler get it guessed? That's why a dinosaur is Easy and a lint roller is Hard: one is unmistakable, the other looks like a paint roller.

Which themes go well together?

For a family evening, Animals, Food & drink and Everyday things. For a cricket-mad crowd, Sports & games with Desi. For a team at work, Office stuff, Tech & gadgets and Corporate slang. In a Drawdle room the host can switch on any mix.

Are these words all right for children?

Most are, but this list wasn't made for them: it has office slang, and a few words like knife and prison. For children, use the words for kids, checked for them and sorted by age.
