Play Pictionary online, free, with friends

Drawdle is a free online Pictionary game for friends, family and teams. One player draws a word, everyone else races to guess it in the chat, and the quickest right answers score the most. Make a room, send the link on WhatsApp or Slack, and play in your browser on a phone or a laptop. No app, no sign-up, no ads.

It is the classic party game Pictionary, without the board, the sand timer or the hunt for a pencil that works: the game keeps time, shows hints and counts the points, so everyone can just draw and laugh.

How to play

  1. Type your name and create a room.
  2. Share the invite link. Up to 30 people can join.
  3. On your turn, pick one of the words offered and draw it.
  4. Everyone else types guesses. The sooner you guess right, the more points you get, and the drawer scores for every right guess.
  5. Everyone draws once each round. Most points after the last round wins.

Letters of the word appear as the clock runs down, so even a hopeless drawing usually ends with somebody getting it. A guess that is nearly right gets a private nudge, and once you have guessed it your messages stay hidden from the players still trying. Full rules, scoring and drawing tips.

Got a crowd? The host can switch the room to Teams: two to four teams take turns to draw, only the drawer's team guesses, and the first right answer wins the points. How team games work.

Who plays Drawdle

Friends on a night in

Everyone on their own phone, in the same room or across cities. One link in the group chat and you are playing.

Families on a video call

Grandparents, cousins and kids together on Zoom, Meet or WhatsApp video, each drawing on their own screen. Easy words keep the youngest in the game.

Remote teams

A fifteen-minute icebreaker for stand-ups, offsites and the office party. Up to 30 colleagues fit in one room: switch it to Teams and play team against team. The corporate slang pack makes managers draw “synergy”.

Classrooms

Load the week's vocabulary as custom words and let students draw them. More ideas for online Pictionary games.

Questions

Is Drawdle free?

Yes. Every game, every word pack, no ads.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows or Mac. On a phone you can add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

How many people can play?

From 2 to 30 in one room. With everyone for themselves it is most fun with 4 to 10; for a bigger group, the host can switch the room to Teams and play in 2 to 4 teams, each with at least 2 players.

Can we play over a video call?

Yes. Everyone opens the invite link on their own device and talks on Zoom, Meet, Microsoft Teams or Discord as usual.

Can I use my own words?

Yes. The host can add up to 500 custom words before the game, and when everyone plays for themselves, the drawer can be allowed to write their own word on their turn.

Will we be playing with strangers?

No. There is no public lobby: only people with your invite link or room code can join, and the host can remove anyone.

What if my internet drops mid-game?

Open the page again and you get your seat back, with your score.
