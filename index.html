Pictionary online, free, with friends

Drawdle is a multiplayer drawing and guessing game. One player draws a word, everyone else races to guess it in the chat. Make a room, send the link on WhatsApp or Slack, and play in your browser on a phone or a laptop. No app, no sign-up, no ads.

How to play

  1. Type your name and create a room.
  2. Share the invite link. Up to 16 people can join.
  3. On your turn, pick one of the words offered and draw it.
  4. Everyone else types guesses. The sooner you guess right, the more points you get, and the drawer scores for every right guess.
  5. Everyone draws once each round. Most points after the last round wins.

Words for every group

Pick the word packs your group will enjoy: everyday things, animals, food and drink, places, nature, sports, tech, jobs, fantasy, actions, office stuff and corporate slang. Set the difficulty from easy doodles to properly abstract.

Or add up to 500 of your own words: Bollywood films, cricketers, office in-jokes, this week's class vocabulary. Play with only your words, or mix them in.

Looking for a skribbl.io alternative?

If you have played skribbl.io, you already know how Drawdle works. Rooms are private to the people you invite, the screen fits a phone even with the keyboard open, the host sets rounds, draw time, hints and words, and nobody has to make an account.

Questions

Is Drawdle free?

Yes. Every game, every word pack, no ads.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

How many people can play?

From 2 to 16 in one room. It is most fun with 4 to 10.

Can we play over a video call?

Yes. Everyone opens the invite link on their own device and talks on Zoom, Meet, Teams or Discord as usual. It works well for remote team games and virtual hangouts.

Can I use my own words?

Yes. The host can add up to 500 custom words before the game, and the drawer can be allowed to write their own word on their turn.
