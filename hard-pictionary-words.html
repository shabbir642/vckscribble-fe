What makes a word hard

A word is rated Hard when a quick drawing can't pin it down. Most are one of three kinds.

  • Ideas with no shape: gravity, destiny, synergy.
  • Lookalikes: is it an antelope or a gazelle? A quiche or a pie? A coaster or a plate?
  • Long phrases: “indian stretchable time”, “single source of truth”.

You can't draw these directly, so you draw around them. Four tricks do most of the work.

Four tricks for drawing hard words

Split it into parts

Draw each part, with a plus sign between them. In Drawdle the blanks above the drawing already show guessers how many words there are.

  • time capsule: a clock, a plus, a pill.
  • photo finish: a camera, a plus, a finish line.
  • shoehorn: a shoe, a plus, a bull's horn.
  • flywheel: a fly, a plus, a wheel.

Draw a sound-alike

When a word won't draw, draw words that sound like it. Guessers say what they see, and someone hears the answer.

  • tapir: a roll of tape, then an ear.
  • antelope: an ant, then a couple running off to get married.
  • metronome: a metro train, then a garden gnome.
  • barnacle: a barn, then a knuckle.

Use symbols and arrows

Arrows show movement and change: draw the before, an arrow, then the after. Simple symbols do the rest, like a heart for love or a lightbulb for an idea.

  • gravity: an apple, an arrow pointing down, a head underneath.
  • evaporation: a puddle, wavy arrows rising from it, a cloud.
  • telepathy: two heads with a zigzag between them.
  • alignment: a row of arrows, all pointing the same way.

Act out a scene

Draw a moment everybody has lived or seen, with stick figures. Verbs, feelings and films work best this way.

  • eavesdrop: someone behind a door, a huge ear pressed against it.
  • hesitate: a figure at the end of a diving board, one foot in the air.
  • small talk: two people by a water cooler, a speech bubble with a sun and a cloud.
  • dilwale dulhania le jayenge: a hand reaching out of a moving train, and someone running to catch it.

One rule for every trick: no letters or numbers. For “3 idiots”, draw three grinning faces in graduation caps.

Questions

Why is something simple like a coaster rated hard?

It's easy to draw and hard to guess: a coaster looks like a plate, a coin or a biscuit. Words are rated Hard when the drawing is easily mistaken for something else, not only when the idea is abstract.

What if nobody can guess it?

In Drawdle, letters of the word appear as the clock runs down, so most hard words fall in the end. The host can allow up to five hint letters and give each drawing up to four minutes.

How do I play only these words online?

Press Play these online at the top. Drawdle opens with these {{count:hard}} words as your next room's own word list, and plays only those. Choosing Hard in a room's settings is different: any word can come up, Hard ones included.

How do you guess hard words faster?

Say what you see, piece by piece: “ant… elope… antelope!” Wrong guesses cost nothing in Drawdle, and a near miss gets a private “is close!” to keep you going. A plus sign or an arrow is the drawer telling you how to read the picture.

Are hard words too hard for children?

Usually, yes: they're abstract ideas, rare animals and office talk. For children, the words for kids, checked for them and sorted by age, work much better. The easy words suit family games across ages.
