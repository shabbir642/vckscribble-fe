How it differs from the board game

In the box game, teams race each other across a board. Drawdle has no board: the drawer picks a word, the guesses are typed into the chat, and the clock and the score are handled for you. Play everyone for themselves, or in teams like the box game.

Letters of the word appear as the time runs down, so even a hopeless drawing usually ends with somebody getting it. Guess early and you score more. With everyone for themselves, the drawer scores too, for every player who gets it right. In teams, only the drawer's team guesses, and the first right answer wins the turn.

What you need

  • A phone, tablet or laptop for each player, with a web browser.
  • The invite link, shared wherever your group talks: WhatsApp, Instagram, Slack, Microsoft Teams.
  • Between 2 and 30 players. Four to ten is the sweet spot with everyone for themselves; with more, play in teams.

No app to install and no accounts. On a phone you can add Drawdle to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Ideas for your next game

Family on a video call

Everyone opens the link on their own device and keeps talking on Zoom, Google Meet or WhatsApp video. Set the difficulty to Easy so the kids can draw every word.

Office team games

Turn on the Corporate slang pack and watch your manager try to draw “synergy” or “circle back”. A game takes about fifteen minutes: a good icebreaker for remote teams. Up to 30 colleagues fit in one room: switch it to Teams and play team against team. For the office Diwali party there are Diwali Pictionary words too.

Classroom revision

Add this week's vocabulary as custom words (at least ten) and play with only those. Drawing a word is a surprisingly good way to remember it.

Desi game night

Switch on the Desi pack for samosas, cricket, Holi, auto rickshaws and Bollywood classics. See the full list of Indian Pictionary words.

Questions

Can you play Pictionary online for free?

Yes. Drawdle is free, with no ads and no account needed.

Can we play in teams?

Yes. In the lobby, the host switches “Everyone for themselves” to “Teams” and picks 2, 3 or 4 teams, each with at least 2 players. The teams take turns to draw, only the drawer's team guesses, and the first right answer wins the turn and the points, while the other teams watch knowing the word. How team games work.

How do I play Pictionary on Zoom or Google Meet?

Keep the call running for the chat and laughs, and have everyone open the Drawdle invite link in their browser. Players on a laptop can put the call and the game side by side.

How long does a game take?

About ten to twenty minutes for most groups. The host can set 1 to 10 rounds and anywhere from 15 seconds to 4 minutes per drawing, and the room settings show roughly how long the game will take.

What words are there?

Fourteen packs, from everyday things and animals to Desi and Corporate slang, in Easy, Mixed or Hard. Or add up to 500 of your own.
